      Time and market

        The three strategies

        Opening range breakout

        Marks the high and low of 9:15–9:30. Buys an ATM call when a 1-minute candle closes above the high, or a put when it closes below the low. Closest to what Shivansh's Fable ran.

        EMA trend cross

        On 5-minute candles, buys a call when the 9 EMA crosses above the 21 EMA and price is above the day's open, or a put on the opposite cross.

        Afternoon range break

        Marks the 12:30–13:00 range and trades its break after 13:00, when expiry-day moves tend to speed up. Shivansh's best Day 2 trade was exactly this kind of break, at 13:23.

        How to read this honestly

        These are simulated fills. Option prices come from a pricing model using India VIX, not real option quotes. Spot checks against real fills from 28 Oct came within a few percent, but expiry-day prices can differ by more.

        No hindsight. The replay reads one minute at a time and acts on the next minute's open, so a strategy can't see the future. Rule changes apply only from the next day and are listed in the changelog.

        Charges are on the high side. Zerodha brokerage, STT at 0.15% on sells, exchange fees, GST and stamp duty, plus 0.5% slippage on every fill.
